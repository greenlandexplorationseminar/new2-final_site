Landslides in GreenlandSisimmasut Kalaallit Nunaanni

The Greenland Ice Sheet is the second largest body of ice in the world, and is one of the largest contributors to sea level rise. As a result of climate change, air and water temperatures have increased over the past few decades, which has led to a net loss of ice mass in Greenland. This loss has been particularly significant in fjords, due to glacial ice coming in contact with warm ocean water. Melting of permafrost and degradation of glaciers weakens terrain and deforms slopes, which can result in loose mud and debris suddenly and rapidly sliding into the ocean, often causing tsunami sized waves to occur and potentially devastate communities. Since the mid-20th century, landslides of increasing magnitude have been documented, particularly in the Vaigat Strait/Nuussuaq/Disko Island region on the west coast of Greenland. This region is highly inhabited, and these natural disasters have been found to cause major damage to infrastructure and way of life for communities in this area.

Kalaallit Nunaata sikuannguata silarsuaani angissuseqarnerpaanit aappaa, imartaata qaqiartornerani annertuumik peqataasuuvoq. Silap pinngortitaata allanngornera pissutigalugu, anorlermut imermullu kissaaneqarnerit ukiuni qulikkuutaani amerlanertigut annikillinngortitsivoq sikummik Kalaallit Nunaanni. Taamaattoq kangerlunni pingaaruteqarnerpaamik takuneqarpoq, sikuit ilisimaneqartut immami kissartumik attaveqarnerat pissutigalugu. Permafrostip aaneranik sikuillu innaallagissaanerat nunap qaqortai sukkasuumik allanngortarpai, taamaalilluni marraq assaanngitsullu qajartuutaannartumik imarmut siaruarsinnaapput, ilaannikkut tsunaminngortitsisarlutik illoqarfinnut ajoquserpassuinngortitsisinnaallutik. 1900-kkut affaanniilli sisimasut annertusiartortut nalunaarsorneqarsimapput, pingaartumik Vaigat/Nuussuaq/Qeqertarsuaq eqqaani, Kalaallit Nunaata kitaani. Tamatuma illoqarfeqarfiginerujusaanera pissutigalugu, pisimasut taama assigisaat innaallagissatigut inuiaqatigiillu inuunertigut ajoqusiisimapput.
